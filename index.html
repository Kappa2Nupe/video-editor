How it works

1. Import – Media tab → ＋ Import. Pick videos, photos, music.
2. Add – tap an item to drop it on the timeline at the white line. Or hold it and drag it onto the timeline.
3. Move around – swipe the timeline left/right to scrub. Pinch (or −/+) to zoom.
4. Edit a clip – tap it. Drag the yellow edges to trim. Hold & drag to move it. ✂ splits at the white line, 🗑 deletes, ↶ undoes.
5. Camera moves – Edit tab → Pan, Tilt, Dolly In/Out, with strength.
6. Intro / Outro – one tap adds a title or end card. Edit words & colors in Edit.
7. Export – makes a video file. Then Save to Photos.

Your project saves automatically on this phone.
